About Me

Passion Meets
Purpose

I am Aanchal Baid, an aspiring Interior Designer with a deep-rooted passion for transforming spaces into meaningful experiences. Currently studying at Dezyne E'cole College — one of India's premier design institutions — I am honing the art of blending functionality with beauty.

My design philosophy is rooted in understanding people first. I approach every project with curiosity, listening to the aspirations of those who inhabit the space, then translating those insights into considered, elegant solutions.

"A well-designed space doesn't just look beautiful — it elevates the way you live, feel, and connect."

From conceptual sketches and mood boards to precise technical drawings and 3D visualizations, I bring a meticulous eye for detail and a love for materials, light, and spatial composition to every project.
